	Once upon a time, in the darkness of the factory, a mistake was made. A cube, twice as large as what should have been made, was created. The conveyer stops immediately, hands grabbed at it. A second later eyes rested upon it. A twitch, and then he flew through the air, into the dumpster. A flick of a lever, and the factory starts up again. The next in line wasn't like the rest of them, different as well. Never infused with its subduing agent, it also became sentient. Maybe they could escape this place. But neither knew what would happen to them if they stayed. One to be immediately used for children's toys.
